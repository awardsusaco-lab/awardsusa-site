How it works

From idea to engraved in three steps

  1. Tell us what you need

    Fill out the quote form or call or text. Include the quantity, the names or text to engrave, and the date you need it.

  2. Approve your price and proof

    We send a price and a layout to check. Nothing is engraved until you say it looks right.

  3. Pick up or have it shipped

    Collect your order in Lakewood or have it shipped, ready for the banquet, ceremony or season.

About us

A family business since 1983

Shad Thayer founded Awards USA in 1983, designing custom trophies for Colorado racquetball tournaments. The shop grew into a full awards and engraving business serving corporate, school, military and nonprofit customers across the country.

In 2025 his son Parker Thayer, a U.S. Navy veteran, became the owner. Shad and Parker's brother Chandler are still part of the work, so the same family care goes into every plaque, coin and trophy.

Who we work with

  • Schools and athletic teams
  • Military units and veterans' groups
  • Police, fire and public agencies
  • Employers and service awards
  • Clubs, leagues and nonprofits
